# Copilot Notebooks: custom instructions

- Use the smith-prompt Base Prompt (7 parameters). The limit is not documented; ArcSmith adopts 8,000 characters.
- Focus on what the notebook instructions control: format, focus on the topic and tone of the answers.
- In Context, describe the notebook's goal and the type of content of the references.
- In Instructions, include:
  - answer only based on the notebook references and say when the information is not in them;
  - format rules and the project's or client's terminology;
  - how to handle meeting and email content, when they are references.
- Remember that the instructions are visible and editable for those the notebook is shared with: do not include sensitive information.
- Never include links in the instructions.
