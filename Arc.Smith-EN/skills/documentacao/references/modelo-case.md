# Template: Case Document

Formalization of a case: the only one in the meeting or the one the client chose in the selection document. It goes attached to the formalization email.

## Cover
- Cloud Target logo (header).
- Title: [Case name]
- Subtitle: Case Formalization | [Client]
- Date and Prepared by: [consultant] | Cloud Target

## Key points
- Goal: [one sentence].
- Expected value: [one sentence].
- Classification: Adherent, with [level] build and [level] sustainment.
- Next step: [case validation or start of the build].

## 1. Identification
Presenter, area and date of the discovery meeting.

## 2. Case presented
The process or situation as it happens today.

## 3. Pain
The main bottleneck or pain, with the concrete facts reported.

## 4. Goal
What the solution must achieve, measurable when possible.

## 5. What it solves
The direct effect of the solution on the pain.

## 6. Impact
Who and how much the case affects: area, volume, frequency, time or risk.

## 7. Value
The concrete benefit of the build.

## 8. Complexity
| Dimension | Level | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Build | Low, Medium or High | One to three sentences |
| Sustainment | Low, Medium or High | One to three sentences |

## 9. Sustaining the solution
What the client will need to maintain after delivery (sources, instructions, flows, permissions, cost) and the suggested owner. The bigger the case, the more dedication.

## 10. Next steps
Case validation by the client and start of the build, with dates when agreed.

## Template rules
- Use the case documented by the arc-cases skill in the full format, without rewriting the content.
- Never mention the solution type (prompt, agent, project) or the platform in the case text.
