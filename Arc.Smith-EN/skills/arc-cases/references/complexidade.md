# Case classification

Each case gets one classification: **Adherent** or **Viable as a project**. An adherent case also gets two complexities, assessed separately: build and sustainment. Combine technical factors (AI requirements, integrations, data) and operational factors (process change, training, maintenance).

## Adherent
The case can be built with the build approach and timeline, with the available data and platforms.

### Build complexity
| Level | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **Low** | Well-bounded use of AI. No complex integrations or major process changes. Accessible, structured data. Local impact and low-risk errors. |
| **Medium** | Needs additional configuration or components, a considerable data volume or a simple integration. May need basic training. Moderate, manageable risks. |
| **High** | Several steps, sources or integrations, or a significant change to the current process. More time to build and validate. Errors in the result can have significant impact. |

### Sustainment complexity
What the client will need to do to keep the solution working after delivery.

| Level | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **Low** | Stable instructions and sources. Rare adjustments, made by the user. No integrations to monitor. |
| **Medium** | Knowledge sources that need periodic updates, instruction tweaks based on usage, a named owner and cost monitoring when there is consumption. |
| **High** | Integrations, flows or connectors to monitor, frequently changing data, governance and permissions to review, several areas involved and a dedicated technical owner. |

When communicating sustainment, say what the client will need to maintain (sources, instructions, flows, permissions, cost) and who should own it. The bigger the case, the more dedication after delivery.

## Viable as a project
The case has value, but the scope is too broad or uncertain for a short build: it needs a formal project, a dedicated team and long-term planning, or depends on systems, data or decisions that do not exist yet. Record the pain, what it solves, the impact and the value, so the client can address it later as a project.

## How the classification guides the decision
- Adherent with low or medium build and sustainment: preferred to deliver value quickly.
- Adherent with high build complexity: consider cutting the scope or building in parts.
- Viable as a project: recorded in the report, never silently discarded.
